# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content creators and operators use maleo while actively planning, generating,
editing, and rendering content. They need to move quickly between creative work
and operational controls without losing project context.

## Product Purpose

maleo turns content intent into editable, traceable production artifacts. The
dashboard should make generation choices, workflow state, and corrective actions
visible so users can produce content quickly without surrendering control.

## Brand Personality

Dense, calm, professional. The interface should feel capable and direct, with
enough information for expert work and no decorative friction.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS dashboards built from repeated cards and decorative gradients.
- Playful controls that obscure standard settings or workflow state.
- Sparse interfaces that hide important generation decisions behind modals.
- Visually loud surfaces that compete with the content being edited.

## Design Principles

- Keep users in flow: common choices stay visible and quick to change.
- Show operational truth: current provider, state, and failures are explicit.
- Prefer familiar controls: standard fields and actions beat novel affordances.
- Keep decisions correctable: important generation choices remain editable.
- Preserve context: settings live near the scope they affect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. All controls must support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
clear labels, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on
color alone to communicate state.
